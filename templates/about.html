About

What is L.S.D.?

The App

L.S.D. is a software specifically designed by a lifter, for other lifters. The app is meant to give as much freedom to the user, while at the same time making the experience as seamless and efficient as can be. This is done by a lot of fancy under the surface work that you might not see as you traverse the app.

These are things like figuring out what your last log was, and then if you have to reload your app, bringing you back to your workout, and then finding out whether the last entry you logged was the last one for that exercise. And if so, bringing you to the next one on the push of a button.

Or like autofilling every text box so you have to enter as little data as possible. Rather than having to fill in every input box, this software autofills the date, and also your last performance for that specific workout, that specific exercise, and that specific set, so that if you only hit one more rep than last time, you only have to change one thing, everything else is already done for you.

The Developer

The developer of this app, Erik Afdahl, is a junior Computer Science and Statistics double major currently enrolled at Gustavus Adolphus college, located in St. Peter, MN.

In regard to why this was built, the reason is simply no other lifting app did everything he wanted, so rather than suffer through the shortcomings of someone else's software, he made his own. And continues to actively use this himself, updating as he sees fit.

Support

This app was not built with the intention of monetization the software at any point, and its only goal is to provide that user experience that other lifting software apps lack. Because of this, there are no features within this app that are behind a paywall. There is no investor board that needs to be satisfied, therefore all creative freedom is in the hands of the developer, and because this is not built with profit in mind, the best user experience is the goal for this app.
