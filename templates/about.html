About

What is L.S.D.?

User Summary

L.S.D. (Lifting Statistical Database) is a smart, friction-free workout tracker designed to completely eliminate the annoyance of manual data entry at the gym. Rather than forcing you to constantly guess your weights or type out repetitive logs, the app uses a custom, context-aware recommendation engine and automated autofill logic to predict your exact performance. By analyzing your historical data, rep ranges, and individual set histories, it automatically populates your weights and target reps in real time, letting you transition seamlessly from set to set. Beyond simple logging, it features advanced time-series analytics, tracking your 1RM, strength streaks (stagnation, progression, and regression), and detailed summary statistics, turning your workout history into a clean, intuitive personal dashboard.

Employer Summary

L.S.D. is a full-stack, data-driven performance analytics platform built using a Python backend, Supabase (PostgreSQL) database, and dynamic web frontend, engineered to solve real-world problems in time-series data tracking and user behavior prediction. The application features a read-optimized relational database architecture that calculates and denormalizes progression metrics—such as 1RM deltas and performance streaks—at the point of ingestion to ensure $O(1)$ query performance. Furthermore, it incorporates a dual-layered algorithmic recommendation engine utilizing Epley inverse functions to handle both linear progressions and statistical edge cases during drastic performance deviations. Demonstrating strong product ownership, full-stack execution, and an ability to leverage modern AI tools effectively for rapid prototyping, the application showcases sophisticated database design, state management, and custom data visualization capabilities.

The Developer

The developer of this app, Erik Afdahl, is a junior Computer Science and Statistics double major currently enrolled at Gustavus Adolphus college, located in St. Peter, MN.

In regard to why this was built, the reason is simply no other lifting app did everything he wanted, so rather than suffer through the shortcomings of someone else's software, he made his own. And continues to actively use this himself, updating as he sees fit.

Support

This app was not built with the intention of monetization the software at any point, and its only goal is to provide that user experience that other lifting software apps lack. Because of this, there are no features within this app that are behind a paywall. There is no investor board that needs to be satisfied, therefore all creative freedom is in the hands of the developer, and because this is not built with profit in mind, the best user experience is the goal for this app.
